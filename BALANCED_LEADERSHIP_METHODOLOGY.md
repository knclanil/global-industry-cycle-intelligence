# Balanced Company Holdings Leadership — USA and Global

## Ranking formula

For companies with scored exposure in **both** sector and factor ETFs:

`Balanced Base = 0.50 × Sector Alignment + 0.50 × Factor Alignment − 0.20 × abs(Sector Alignment − Factor Alignment)`

`ETF Breadth Bonus = 10 × ln(1 + distinct ETFs holding company) / ln(1 + eligible ETFs in regional universe)`

`Final Score = min(100, max(0, Balanced Base + ETF Breadth Bonus))`

Sector/Factor Alignment: within each ETF, company weight is divided by the ETF's 90th-percentile holding weight (capped at 1), multiplied by that ETF's verified performance score (0–100), and averaged across the company's scored ETFs in the category. This measures **performance-aligned ETF ownership**, not company stock-price performance.

Companies lacking a scored sector OR factor exposure are shown as **Insufficient Coverage**, with no Final Score and no Rank. Partial ETF score coverage is marked **Partial** and remains provisional. The `base_score` column retains the previous unpenalized mean for comparison; `balanced_base_score` and `consistency_penalty` show the new method. The previous formula is not used for ranking.

USA uses only USA sector/factor ETF holdings and scores; Global uses only Global sector/factor ETF holdings and scores. No company-level holdings are included in the knowledge graph. ETF scores must be supplied in `data/holdings/etf_performance_scores.csv` (see `data/holdings/ETF_PERFORMANCE_SCORES_README.md`); this package does **not** automatically derive those scores from rotation data. Existing ranking parquet snapshots must be regenerated by the holdings refresh before the UI reflects the new formula.
