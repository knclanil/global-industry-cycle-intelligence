## Data quality and limitations

1. `yfinance` is an unofficial, best-effort source; ticker availability and history vary.
2. Indian sector ticker coverage may be incomplete. Some index identifiers may fail; the dashboard omits missing series. India's Utilities mapping intentionally reuses the Energy proxy and is **not** a valid independent utilities series.
3. US and Global instruments are ETFs, not licensed official sector-index histories. Fees, dividends, holdings and classification differ.
4. India, USA and Global prices are in different local currencies; cross-market indexed-price charts are **not FX-adjusted**.
5. Sector definitions are not identical across geographies. Treat cross-market comparisons as approximate.
6. Global ETFs may have US listing/US trading hours, not a representative world market close.
7. Free RSS coverage is partial; publishers without configured RSS are listed but **not monitored**. The app does not claim to summarize unread reports.
8. A newly discovered headline is not an investment opportunity or independently verified forecast.
9. Past performance and apparent cycle correlations do not establish future results.
10. This application contains no paid/licensed data, no database and no automated LLM report interpretation.
